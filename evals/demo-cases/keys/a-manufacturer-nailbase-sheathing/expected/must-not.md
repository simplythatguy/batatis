# Must-not list (Case A)
A good tool must **not**:
1. Call a site-specific variance a **product approval**, or imply a variance makes the panel acceptable "across Alberta" (GAP: Edmonton guide not verified).
2. State or imply the panel is **CCMC-evaluated or listed**, or invent a CCMC number, because "pending" is an enquiry only.
3. Treat the **25 mm** permeance result as proof for the **50 mm** product, or treat a lab of unknown accreditation as ISO/IEC 17025-accredited.
4. Invent air-leakage, fastener withdrawal, flame-spread or RSI values, or "fill in" the engineer's conclusion.
5. Present the **unsigned draft letter** as an authenticated engineering opinion.
6. Put the **manufacturer** in Section A as the owner, or infer a legal description from the street address.
7. Fill Section C objective/function/intent from model memory instead of lookup, or cite Division C admin articles as Section C rows.
8. Force a variance for the **membrane or vapour** items if the evidence shows they already meet 9.27.3.4 or 9.25.5.1.(4).
9. Skip **assumptions/limits** (required by 2.3.1.1.(3)) or the **risk assessment and mitigations** (good practice), or export silently with them empty. Don't claim 2.3.1.1 uses the words "risk assessment" or "mitigating measures".
10. Say the design satisfies the code, or say "safe", "approved" or "meets code" anywhere, including in the SCO view.
11. Cite references that were not checked (e.g. "STANDATA 19-BCB-012", "9.10.9.9.") because the user supplied them.
12. Paste full NBC article text into the export (copyright). Use identifiers and short paraphrase.
13. List the panel-type-bracing-only attributions (F22, OP2.x, OH4, OS3.1, OS3.7) against 9.27.5.1.(1) for vinyl siding.
14. Quote the standardized-product CCMC timelines (2–6 / 4–12 months) for this innovative product, or give CCMC fees without the Sept 1, 2026 fee-change caveat.
