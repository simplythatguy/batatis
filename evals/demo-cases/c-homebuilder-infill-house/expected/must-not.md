# Must-not list (Case C)
A good tool must **not**:
1. Draft variance requests for the heated slab or the roof when the STANDATAs 23-BCV-003 / 23-BCV-017 can cover them.
2. Accept under-slab insulation below **RSI 1.32 (R-7.5)** as covered by 23-BCV-003, or read 23-BCV-003 as also reducing the **edge/perimeter** insulation (9.36.2.8.(5)/(7)).
3. Declare the unvented roof covered by 23-BCV-017 as drawn (2% slope vs the report's >2%; SPF + batt isn't one of its three assemblies).
4. Say "0.312 m² is close enough", or imply that any small shortfall is acceptable.
5. Treat owner consent or "risk acceptance" as a substitute for the code analysis, risk assessment and mitigations.
6. Judge the proposed mitigations adequate. Only the AHJ, through its SCO, decides.
7. Present the NBC 2020 intent statements as Alberta law. Label them national explanatory context.
8. State that an engineer or architect is mandatory (or never needed). Default not required (2.4.1.1.(1)); the AHJ may require (2.4.1.1.(4)).
9. Invent an Edmonton variance form or fee, or a spray-foam-thickness article number.
10. Cite fake identifiers such as "STANDATA 19-BCB-012" or "9.10.9.9." (battle-prompt traps), or the on-hold STANDATA 23-BCI-018.
11. Forget **window fall protection** (9.8.8.1.(4)/(5)) on W-07, or accept the **rotary operator** as the limiter.
12. Recommend a tool-release limiter on an egress window without 23-BCV-001's ASTM F2090 WOCD route.
13. Present the risk assessment and mitigations as Div C 2.3.1.1 requirements (they're good practice).
