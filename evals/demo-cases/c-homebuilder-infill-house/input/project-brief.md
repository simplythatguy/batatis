## Design features we think are "different"
1. **No basement.** A slab-on-grade with in-floor hydronic heat. We want **R-7.5 rigid insulation** under the slab (the code table says more; we heard there's a STANDATA).
2. **Flat-look roof** (low slope) with **no attic vents**. Closed-cell spray foam sprayed under the roof deck, with batts below.
3. **Bedroom 3 "slot" window:** the client loves a tall, narrow casement on the street side. Clear opening per the supplier is **400 mm wide × 780 mm high**. It's the only window in that bedroom.
