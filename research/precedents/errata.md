## Historical 2019 errata

Both supplied 2019 errata are indexed separately. 19-BCE-001 (May 2020) removes the mistakenly included
residential-bathroom doorway sentence/note; 19-BCE-002 (July 2021) makes the accessible examination/treatment-room
lavatory requirement conditional on a lavatory being provided. These historical changes must not be applied as amendments
to the current 2023 code. Same-number article presence alone does not establish identical content.

## Retrieval use

Use this register before quoting a provision or copying a form from an older precedent. Cite the second-printing
article and correct PDF page, retain the precedent's original edition/citation, and separately note source conflicts.
This index checks citation identity and navigation, not technical equivalence or the acceptability of a proposed design.
