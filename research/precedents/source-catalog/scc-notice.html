Notice : variances under the Safety Codes Act [2019]

  • Summary
  • Detailed Information
  • Related (0)
Description

Explains a variance under the Safety Codes Act as a power exercised by a safety codes officer or an Administrator under the Act which provides equivalent or greater safety performance to persons and property as provided by the Act, and explains why a variance might be issued.
