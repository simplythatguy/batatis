Using the board to hold the siding

NBC(AE) 2023 · Div B · Sentence 9.23.17.3.(1)
Gypsum sheathing, rigid insulation and fibreboard shall not be used for the attachment of cladding materials.
NBC(AE) 2023 p2, Div B, Sentence 9.23.17.3.(1) [PDF p.990] · Reproduced with permission of the NRC
Why this rule exists

To limit the application of Sentence 9.23.17.2.(1) and Table 9.23.17.2.-A so as to exclude sheathing materials that do not have an adequate capacity to hold fasteners.
