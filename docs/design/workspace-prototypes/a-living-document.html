Proposed alternativeOn wall type W1, vertical vinyl siding is fastened with ring-shank nails at 400 mm o.c. directly into the 6 mm fibre-cement facer of a 50 mm insulating sheathing panel, instead of into framing, furring or a listed sheathing.

Your case Appendix

Why a variance

The benchmark is Sentence 9.27.5.1.(2): cladding fastened to sheathing of the kind and thickness it lists. Our panel replaces that sheathing, so the siding is held by the facer alone.

✦

Risk

If the facer cannot hold the nails, siding could loosen in high wind and falland detach from the wall, mainly at corners and upper storeys.

Mitigating measures

Nails at 400 mm o.c., ring-shank only; installer follows the manufacturer's guide; site check before cladding.
