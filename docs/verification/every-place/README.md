## Questions judges ask

- **Does it work outside Calgary and Edmonton?** It names the right office everywhere (435 of
  435 places), but it prepares applications only for Calgary and Edmonton (2 of
  2), whose own steps it has checked. Everywhere else it says so and who to ask
  (433 of 433 places) instead of guessing at an office's forms: the research
  found cities with their own variance forms (Lethbridge, Medicine Hat) and others that publish nothing.
- **What about Vancouver, Toronto or Montréal?** Not covered, and Batatis says so: it names the code that applies there
  and refuses the final Alberta application.
- **How do you know the office is right?** The expected office comes from the Safety Codes Council accreditation
  snapshot and the research table, not from the files the app reads, and a test fails if those files drift.
- **Could it invent an office?** No. The office is a lookup in sourced data. A name Batatis does not know gets the
  general Alberta answer (accredited municipality, or the Alberta Safety Codes Authority) and no final application,
  never a made-up office.
- **How is a city added?** Read its own variance steps and forms from its official sources and write them into a pack
  (`engine/data/jurisdictions/packs/<id>.json`, like Calgary's); the place is then covered. A generated pack is never
  covered.
- **Can I see it run?** `.venv/bin/python scripts/every_place_proof.py --quick` checks one place of every kind in about
  a minute; without `--quick` it checks every place.

## How the checks work

The engine runs in-process through its real API routes (`/gaps`, `/export`, `/submit`), the same code the app
calls. The application is the engine's synthetic sample (`engine/export/sample.py`: continuous exterior
insulation on a Part 9 house, five Division B provisions, synthetic attachments). Only the place changes.
`engine/tests/test_every_place.py` runs one place of every kind on every engine test run; this script runs all.

Sources of truth: `docs/research/2026-10-28-every-jurisdiction/alberta-scc-snapshot.json` (Safety Codes Council
accreditation search, 2026-10-29) and `alberta-jurisdictions.csv` (390 places with their sources). The place files the
app reads are generated from them by `python -m engine.jurisdictions.places_build`, and a test fails if they drift.

## What this does not show

- Code identification accuracy itself. The place cannot change it (check 5), and the research measured it on 653 real
  cases by place: `docs/research/2026-10-28-every-jurisdiction/evidence/evidence.md`.
- Local intake steps and fees outside Calgary and Edmonton. That is why Batatis does not prepare applications there:
  it names the office, links its sources and says who to ask.
- Hamlets and unlisted localities. A name Batatis does not know gets the general Alberta answer and no final
  application.
