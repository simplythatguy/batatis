Citation/quote checks do not prove that the complete requirement, every table/standard/exception, or every article needed by a proposal was captured. PDFs were checked as source text, without a rendered-layout or diagram audit. No professional or legal review is represented.

Use results as source-citation retrieval evidence for administrative completeness. They do not establish technical transfer, an exhaustive relevance gold set, legal applicability, calibration, design compliance, safety or professional approval.
