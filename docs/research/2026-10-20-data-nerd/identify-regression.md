## Product approval vs variance (explainer card)

A product approval and a variance answer different questions. Product approval is market-wide: a CCMC evaluation report, or one from an evaluator the Provincial Building Administrator has approved, can be used anywhere in Alberta to show the product meets the code. A variance (alternative solution) is a decision about one building or site: a safety codes officer accepts it when it gives about the same or better safety, documents it under Division C 2.3.1, and registers it with the Safety Codes Council. A province-wide STANDATA variance from the Provincial Building Administrator is the exception, but it needs a province-wide issue, not one company's product. So a manufacturer wanting "approval across Alberta" needs an evaluation report; a pilot house can go ahead on a site variance in the meantime.

- CCMC (NRC) evaluates construction products for code compliance to help manufacturers get market acceptance. [NRC, Canadian Construction Materials Centre (web page)](https://nrc.canada.ca/en/certifications-evaluations-standards/canadian-construction-materials-centre) "The Canadian Construction Materials Centre (CCMC) assesses compliance with Canadian building, energy and safety codes."
- Alberta recognises CCMC evaluation reports, and those of organizations approved by the Provincial Building Administrator, as a way to show code compliance. NBC(AE) 2023 2nd printing, Div A 1.2.2.1.(2), PDF p.38: "Evaluation reports issued by the Canadian Construction Materials Centre … or an organization approved by the Provincial Building Administrator may be used in determining compliance" — © National Research Council of Canada. Source: https://nrc-publications.canada.ca/eng/view/ft/?id=0316d953-0d55-4311-af69-cad55efec499
- The PBA approves other evaluators by STANDATA (e.g. QAI Laboratories, 23-BCI-005; also 23-BCI-006, -007, -013, -040 per the local STANDATA set). [STANDATA 23-BCI-005, Approved organization - QAI Laboratories (May 2024), p.1](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/cb3d1662-1354-45c8-aab8-29b91f2a6c35/resource/57cebce9-5fe6-4583-a0bd-f2e4c7eedec9/download/ma-23-bci-005-approved-organization-qai-laboratories.pdf) "This interpretation recognizes QAI Laboratories as an approved organization that issues evaluation reports that may be used to determine compliance … NBC(AE)."
- A province-wide variance is requested from the Provincial Building Administrator, applies everywhere in Alberta, and must address a province-wide issue; these are published as STANDATA variances (23-BCV series). NBC(AE) 2023 2nd printing, Div C 2.2.1.2.(1), PDF p.729: "A written request for a variance may be submitted to the Provincial Building Administrator."; NBC(AE) 2023 2nd printing, Div C Note A-2.2.1.2., PDF p.743: "The application to the Provincial Building Administrator must show that the issue is province-wide."; [STANDATA 23-BCV-001, Window opening control devices (2024-04), p.3](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/08ac69ae-5639-46a3-83d2-c52246c78621/resource/01b622f0-f4a1-426d-b15f-ffc25e725949/download/ma-standata-variance-building-23-bcv-001-2024-04.pdf) "This VARIANCE is applicable throughout the Province of Alberta."
- A site-specific variance is issued by a safety codes officer (or an Administrator) when the alternative gives approximately equivalent or greater safety; the officer must notify an Administrator. [Safety Codes Act, RSA 2000 c S-1, s.38(1), p.31](https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/documents/Acts/S01.pdf) "An Administrator or a safety codes officer may issue a written variance … of the opinion that the variance provides approximately equivalent or greater safety performance"; [Safety Codes Act s.38(3), p.31](https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/documents/Acts/S01.pdf) "A safety codes officer on issuing a variance shall notify an Administrator."
- For building, the officer must document the alternative solution per Division C 2.3.1 and send each variance to the Safety Codes Council within 10 days. [SCC Information Bulletin IB-2025-11-18, Mandatory Documentation of Variances, p.2](https://ebs.safetycodes.ab.ca/documents/webdocs/SR/IB-2025-11-18%20Mandatory%20Documentation%20of%20Variances.pdf) "Submit a copy of each variance to the Safety Codes Council within 10 days of issuance."; [IB-2025-11-18, p.2](https://ebs.safetycodes.ab.ca/documents/webdocs/SR/IB-2025-11-18%20Mandatory%20Documentation%20of%20Variances.pdf) "Document alternative solutions as required by Division C of the applicable code."
- From December 1, 2026 variances are registered through the SCC Orders and Variances Portal; registration is not Council approval. [SCC Information Bulletin IB-2026-10-01, Orders and Variances Registration Changes, p.2](https://ebs.safetycodes.ab.ca/documents/webdocs/SR/IB-2026-10-01%20Orders%20and%20Variances%20Registration%20Changes.pdf) "Variances must be registered in OVP at the time of issuance or as soon as reasonably practicable thereafter."; [IB-2026-10-01, p.1](https://ebs.safetycodes.ab.ca/documents/webdocs/SR/IB-2026-10-01%20Orders%20and%20Variances%20Registration%20Changes.pdf) "Registration of an order or variance through OVP does not constitute Council approval of that order or variance"
- The Act lets an Administrator record a variance and the location it applies to (variance register). [Safety Codes Act s.62, p.51](https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/documents/Acts/S01.pdf) "An Administrator may place an entry on the relevant information system that briefly indicates the subject-matter of a variance and the location …"
