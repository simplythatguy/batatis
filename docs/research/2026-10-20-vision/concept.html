Pre-flight check

Batatis prepares and checks the application. It never says a design is safe or compliant; professionals and the Safety Codes Officer decide.

How it works

Models propose; deterministic code and people decide. Each stage says who is trusted for it. Results use the NRC’s four states: Meets, Does not meet, Information not available, Uncertain.

Where Alberta’s variances actually are

Five Part 9 fire-protection articles make up 81% of building-article variances registered in Alberta in the first half of 2026. All five turn on things the drawings show: limiting distance, exposing building faces, soffits, party walls, and ducts crossing rated assemblies.
